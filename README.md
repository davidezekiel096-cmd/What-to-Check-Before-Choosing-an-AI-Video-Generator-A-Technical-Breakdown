# What to Check Before Choosing an AI Video Generator: A Technical Breakdown

## Summary

Choosing an AI video generator shouldn't be based on a single impressive demo clip alone. This breakdown covers the specific, checkable factors that actually determine whether a platform fits a real content workflow.

## Technical Checklist for Evaluating an AI Video Generator

### 1. Model Architecture Variety
- Text-to-video (new scene generation from written description)
- Image-to-video (animating an existing still image)
- Cinematic (optimized for dramatic camera movement, film-grade output)
- Stylized (anime, abstract, retro — illustrated aesthetics)

Confirm the platform genuinely offers multiple model categories within one workspace, not a single model marketed under different preset names.

### 2. Consistency Mechanism
For any project spanning multiple clips, series content or branded avatars, confirm whether the platform offers a genuine consistency feature (typically reference-image-anchored) versus independent, unrelated generations per clip.

### 3. Generation Speed and Resolution Tiers
- Confirm average generation time
- Confirm resolution options and which plan tier unlocks each (720p / 1080p / 4K is a common pattern)

### 4. Export Format and Aspect Ratio Support
- File formats: MP4, WebM, GIF, MOV
- Aspect ratios: 16:9 (widescreen), 9:16 (vertical/social), 1:1 (square/feed), 21:9 (cinematic ultrawide)

### 5. Structured Templates / Blueprints
Pre-built prompt templates reduce the prompt-engineering burden for less experienced users, since effective prompts generally require specifying subject, environment, camera movement, and style together.

### 6. Ownership and Training Data Terms
- Confirm who owns generated output, and under what plan tier
- Confirm whether prompts/uploads are used for AI training, and whether consent is required

## FAQ

**Why does genuine model variety matter more than one polished model?**
Different generation tasks (new scenes, animating stills, stylized output) benefit from different model architectures, so a single general-purpose model can't optimally handle every use case.

**What's the technical basis for character consistency features?**
A reference image anchors identity-relevant features across otherwise independent generation runs, allowing a character or avatar to remain visually consistent across a multi-scene series.

**Should resolution tier be a major factor in platform choice?**
Yes, if final output needs to meet a specific resolution standard for its publishing destination, confirm which plan tier actually unlocks that resolution before committing.

**What ownership terms should be verified before commercial use?**
Explicit confirmation that generated outputs are owned by the creator with commercial licensing, and that prompts/uploads aren't used for AI training without consent.
